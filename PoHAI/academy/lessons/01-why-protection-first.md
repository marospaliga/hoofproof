# Lesson 1 — Why protection first

**Every decision in HoofProof hangs off one question: is this cow actually protected?**

A cow in this system is not a logo or a pledge. A cow is *Active* only when
people have put real rating behind it and the herd can prove recent care:
either a care quorum, documented labor hours, or an open funding need — all
inside the chain's freshness window. This is the **protection anchor**. No
anchor, no mints, no ETH releases. Everything else is decoration on that
foundation.

The windows are set by the chain, not by you. On the production clock a proof
counts for 90 days; on a beta clock the same windows are compressed so a
tester can cross them in a session. The app always shows the clock it is
actually running on — never a hardcoded number.

## What the rating actually means

The rating you see on a cow is not popularity, and it is not a score that is
handed out. It is **rating that was spent**. Every point of rating was earned
by real work — an attested hour of care, a passed course, a need met — and
when you rate something, you put some of it behind it.

Three rules make that meaningful:

1. **A vote is rating spent.** There is no size ladder and no weighting
   formula: rating a cow harder simply means spending more rating. So a high
   rating is not a popularity contest — it is a pile of somebody's earned
   reputation.
2. **Your rating is the budget.** Rating you commit is *locked* against the
   cow or the post you rated. It is a real lien on your Stasis, not a shadow
   counter: you cannot spend the same rating twice, and you cannot bond a
   labor claim with rating you have already committed. Spend rating you do
   not have free and the vote fails — nothing moves.
3. **No dilution, no momentum.** Nobody's old ratings are watered down
   because a new voter showed up, and nobody's vote counts extra for being
   active. One unit of rating, one unit of voice. That is why the feed
   breathes like Steemit: nothing here is a cheap "+1".

Ratings matter because they decide three things:

1. **Activation** — a cow needs 1,000 weighted stake (rating committed to it,
   worth more the longer it stays committed) plus a live protection anchor to
   go from Inert to Active. Stakes are commitment.
2. **Labor value** — an hour of attested work is worth more on a well-rated
   cow (up to 3× the base rate). Rating steers work toward where care is
   already proven.
3. **Moderation** — hidden or restored content is decided by your
   reputation, the score you have earned. The field polices itself with its
   own reputation.

## Protection is the gate, not the goal

Stasis (reputation) and ETH only ever trace back to protection performance:
labor hours attested, needs reduced, care proven. There is no yield, no APY,
no speculation. If someone offers you "returns" on HoofProof, they have
misunderstood the system — or they are trying to trick you.
