# Lesson 1 — Why protection first

**Every decision in HoofProof hangs off one question: is this cow actually protected?**

A cow in this system is not a logo or a pledge. A cow is *Active* only when a
real person has put up a bond and the herd can prove recent care: either a
care quorum, documented labor hours, or an open funding need — all within the
last 90 days. This is the **protection anchor**. No anchor, no mints, no ETH
releases. Everything else is decoration on that foundation.

## What the rating actually means

The rating you see on a cow is not popularity. It is a **zero-sum voice
field**: every voter has a fixed budget of *voice*, locked against the posts
and evidence they support. When you spend voice on one thing, it is locked
there — you cannot spend the same voice twice. If a whale votes over their
budget, their *oldest* locks dilute first. This is why the feed breathes like
Steemit: nothing is a cheap "+1".

Ratings matter because they decide three things:

1. **Activation** — a cow needs 1,000 weighted stake (not votes — stakes) to
   go from Inert to Active. Stakes are commitment; votes are cheer.
2. **Labor value** — an hour of attested work is worth more on a well-rated
   cow (up to 3×). Rating steers help toward where it is most proven.
3. **Moderation** — hidden or restored content is decided by rating weight.
   The field polices itself with its own reputation.

## Protection is the gate, not the goal

Stasis (reputation) and ETH only ever trace back to protection performance:
labor hours attested, needs reduced, care proven. There is no yield, no APY,
no speculation. If someone offers you "returns" on HoofProof, they have
misunderstood the system — or they are trying to trick you.
